MMA Dispensing Gun

Professional-grade dispensing gun designed for precise, controlled application of two-component MMA (Methyl Methacrylate) adhesive from 50 mL or 400 mL cartridges. Provides smooth and consistent material dispensing while reducing waste and ensuring accurate adhesive application.

Key Features

  • Precise Control: Enables controlled, consistent application of two-component MMA adhesive
  • Dual Cartridge Compatibility: Works with both 50 mL and 400 mL side-by-side cartridges
  • Smooth Dispensing: Delivers uniform material flow for reliable, even bead lines
  • Reduced Waste: Controlled trigger action minimizes excess adhesive use
  • Professional-Grade Build: Designed for repeated, reliable use in demanding work environments
  • Accurate Application: Supports consistent mix ratio delivery for dependable bond quality

Suitable Applications

  • Dispensing two-component MMA structural adhesives, such as CoreBond GP-0416
  • Structural bonding of engineering plastics and composite assemblies
  • Metal bonding applications requiring consistent adhesive flow
  • Industrial, workshop, and field assembly and repair work
