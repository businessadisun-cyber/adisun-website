Rust Seal

Rust Seal: Rust Conversion Agent is a high-performance, water-based acrylic formula engineered to neutralize rust and transform corroded surfaces into a stable, paintable layer. Ideal for industrial, automotive, marine, and household applications.

Key Features

  • Converts rust into a protective barrier
  • Water-based & eco-friendly
  • Non-flammable & safe for indoor use
  • Easy application: brush, spray, or roller
  • Fast drying & long-lasting performance

Applications

  • Automotive chassis, underbody, and panels
  • Industrial machinery and metal structures
  • Marine equipment and ship hulls
  • Household tools, fences, and metal furniture
  • DIY restoration and maintenance projects
