PuroClean

Puro-Clean is a high-performance acid-based cleaner formulated to remove stubborn construction residues and mineral deposits. Its deep-penetrating action targets contaminants like cement, saltpeter, grout, lime scale, and rust, restoring surfaces to their natural brilliance.

Key Features

  • Heavy-Duty Cleaning: Dissolves cement scale, lime scale, rust, and hard water stains
  • Surface Versatility: Ideal for ceramic, porcelain, terracotta, aged marble & stoneware
  • Time-Saving: Minimizes manual scrubbing with rapid action
  • Aesthetic Renewal: Enhances brightness and clarity of treated surfaces
  • Residue-Free Finish: Leaves surfaces pristine and refreshed
  • Mold Prevention: Helps reduce microbial growth and odors

Suitable Applications

  • Residential bathrooms, kitchens, patios
  • Commercial facilities, lobbies, washrooms
  • Post-construction surface cleaning
  • Restoration of aged or stained tile and stone surfaces
