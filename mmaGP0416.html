CoreFix — MMA Structural Adhesives

CoreBond GP-0416

CoreBond GP-0416 is a third-generation, general-purpose, fast-cure, two-part methyl methacrylate (MMA) structural adhesive engineered for bonding engineering plastics and composite assemblies with little to no surface preparation, and for bonding all metals without a primer.

GP-0416 delivers a unique combination of high strength, excellent fatigue endurance, outstanding impact resistance, and superior toughness — built with the most advanced formulation technology and manufacturing process available.

Key Features

  • Fast Cure: 4–6 minute working time and 14–15 minute fixture time at 24°C
  • Wide Operating Range: Performs from -40°C to 140°C
  • Gap Filling Capacity: Bonds gaps up to 4 mm
  • Minimal Surface Prep: Bonds engineering plastics and composites with little to no surface preparation
  • No Primer on Metals: Bonds all metals without primer needed
  • High-Performance Bonding: Excellent fatigue endurance, impact resistance, and toughness
  • Chemical Resistance: Excellent resistance to acids and bases (3–10 pH), hydrocarbons, and salt solutions

Suitable Applications

  • Engineering Plastics: ABS, Acrylics, Polycarbonate, PVC
  • FRP: Epoxy-based, Polyester-based, Vinyl Ester-based, PU-based composites
  • Metals (with Primer-MNM): Aluminum, CRS (Cold Rolled Steel), Stainless Steel and many others
  • Structural bonding of composite and engineering plastic assemblies
