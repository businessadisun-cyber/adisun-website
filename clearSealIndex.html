Clear Seal

Clear Seal is a high-performance, water-based transparent waterproofing resin designed to protect building surfaces from water ingress. Formulated with pure acrylic emulsion, UV-resistant additives, and advanced polymers, it forms a seamless, colorless membrane that adheres strongly to a wide range of substrates. Ideal for both new and old construction, Clear Seal offers long-lasting protection without altering the appearance of the surface.

Key Features

  • Transparent and colourless finish
  • High solid content (>85%) for durable protection
  • Excellent water resistance (0.3 MPa, 30 min – no leakage)
  • Strong adhesion to varied substrates including tile, concrete, wood, and metal
  • Flexible at low temperatures (no cracks at -40 °C)
  • Water-based and safe for potable water projects
  • Excellent weather resistance and UV stability
  • Easy brush or roller application

Applications

  • Tile floors, concrete surfaces, interior rooms
  • Bathrooms, kitchens, basements, roof slabs
  • Cement walls, window sills, wood, foam board, iron
